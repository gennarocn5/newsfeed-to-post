Newsfeed-To-Post

Privacy

Last updated November 2026.

What the app accesses in Buffer

When you connect Buffer, Newsfeed-To-Post asks for four permissions: account:read (your organizations and connected channels), posts:write (to add article posts to your queue or drafts), ideas:write (to add post ideas to your Ideas board) and offline_access (so the connection lasts without signing in again every hour). It cannot read your past posts or your analytics.

Feeds and articles

The feeds you add and the list of articles you've already handled are saved only in your browser's local storage. To read a feed or an article, the app's server fetches the public page and passes it to your browser. Nothing is stored or logged on the server.

AI post ideas

When you click Write 3 ideas, the article's headline, excerpt, text (or the full text you paste) and your voice notes are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to write the ideas. Anthropic does not use API inputs to train its models. Nothing is sent to the AI until you click the button.

Where your data lives

Your Buffer access tokens are stored only in an encrypted, HTTP-only cookie in your browser. Page scripts can't read them, and they are not saved on our servers. The app has no database, uses no analytics or advertising trackers, and does not sell or share any data.

Removing access

Click Disconnect in the app to clear your session. You can also revoke the app's access in your Buffer account settings. Clearing your browser's site data removes your saved feeds.
