Newsfeed-To-Post

For newsrooms, publishers & media brands

Every new story,
ready to post.

Connect your site's feed. Each article you publish becomes a ready-to-go post built from your own headline and excerpt, plus three fresh angles written by AI from the story itself. One click sends them to your Buffer queue and Ideas board.

Connect Buffer Works with any RSS, Atom or JSON feed. Privacy
  1. 01

    Add your feed

    Paste your site's address. The app finds your RSS or Atom feed and lists new articles in an inbox.

  2. 02

    Review the posts

    An article post shaped to each network's limit, plus three AI angles built from a stat, a quote or a person in the story.

  3. 03

    Send to Buffer

    The article post goes to your queue or drafts. The ideas land on your Ideas board, ready for your team.
